# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

**Avengers: Endgame** is a superhero film produced by *Marvel Studios* and directed by the Russo Brothers.  
It was released in 2019 and serves as the conclusion to the Infinity Saga.

## Main Characters
- Iron Man (Tony Stark)
- Captain America (Steve Rogers)
- Thor
- Black Widow
- Hulk
- Hawkeye

## Why I Like It
I like **Avengers: Endgame** because it combines action, emotion, and teamwork.  
The story highlights the importance of **sacrifice** and **hope** even in difficult times.
